Sidebet is an AI bookie that lives in your iMessage group chat. It turns trash talk into yes/no markets, sets the odds by reading your chat's history, and tracks every bet on a live chart. Public events mirror real Kalshi odds and settle on their own.

How it works

From a hot take to a payout in four messages.

Scan

Sidebet reads the group's history once and writes a compact dossier and a profile for each person. Markets read ~11K tokens instead of ~115K.

Open

Someone types !new, or Sidebet spots a bold claim. Claude sets the opening odds and cites the chat for each side.

Bet

Friends bet with !yes and !no. Odds blend the AI's prior with the money, and Sidebet reacts when someone posts real evidence.

Settle

The creator resolves it, or Kalshi does for public events. Winners split the pot, and Sidebet posts who paid who.
